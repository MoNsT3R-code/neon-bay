## 🌐 Overview

Pepper has just stepped off the bus in Neon Bay, a sun-soaked city of neon hotels, palm trees and very fast cars. Borrow any ride, fly the Sky Hopper, race boats round the harbour, tear up Sunset Ridge in a thunderstorm, and become a legend.

**Version 2.0** adds 8 story missions, weather, a phone, a helicopter, boats and a pool hall.

## ✨ Key Features

✅ **Story and side missions** - Yellow markers are story missions, blue markers are pizza jobs, and purple is the street race.

✅ **Drive anything** - Borrow any car you find, take taxi fares, fly the Sky Hopper helicopter, and race boats at the marina.

✅ **Wanted system** - Cops chase you if you cause trouble. Stealing cars near police, ramming them or hitting people raises your wanted stars.

✅ **Nitro and combos** - Chain drifts, near misses and big air to earn cash. Drifting refills your nitro.

✅ **Shops and garage** - Outfits and hats at Drip Boutique, cars at Turbo Motors, and tuning and saves at Rafi's Garage.

✅ **In-game phone** - Contacts, weather, places, map and camera in one place.

✅ **Things to do** - The arcade, Hoop Court, the Neon Cue Club pool hall, Taco Comet, the Neon Needle, Stunt Park and Sunset Ridge.

✅ **Collectibles** - 15 hidden cassette tapes and stunt ramps that award airtime bonuses.

✅ **Photo mode** - Take pictures with filters.

✅ **Living world** - Dynamic weather, a day and night clock, radio music and save games.

## 🚀 How to Play

Clone or download this repository, then open `index.html` in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

```bash
git clone https://github.com/MoNsT3R-code/neon-bay.git
cd neon-bay
# Open index.html in your browser
```

## 🎮 Controls

### On Foot

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| Sprint | `Shift` |
| Jump | `Space` |
| Look around | Drag the mouse |
| Enter a car | `F` |
| Use shops and garage | `E` |

### Driving

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Gas / brake | `W` / `S` |
| Steer | `A` / `D` |
| Handbrake drift | `Space` |
| Nitro | `N` |
| Horn | `H` |
| Radio | `R` |
| Taxi fares | `T` |
| Change camera (chase, hood, cinematic, top-down) | `C` |

### Sky Hopper (Helicopter)

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Fly forward / back | `W` / `S` |
| Turn | `A` / `D` |
| Up / down | `Space` / `Shift` |

### General

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Phone (also answers calls) | `Q` |
| Map | `M` |
| Photo mode | `P` |
| Pause | `Esc` |

## 💡 Tips

- To lose your wanted stars, break line of sight with the cops or drive into the Spray Shack.
- Drifting refills your nitro.
- Crashing drops your combo, so bank it before you hit anything.
